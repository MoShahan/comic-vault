Why & when

Built for reading on your own device

Comics stay on your phone. Progress and preferences live in local SQLite. Mark as read is always a conscious choice.

Why use it

  • Local CBZ / ZIP library — no uploads, no accounts
  • Cover grid with continue reading and filters
  • Reader controls: scrubber, zoom, dimmer, keep awake
  • Privacy by design — no analytics or cloud sync

When to use it

  • You already keep comics as CBZ files on your phone
  • You want offline reading with saved page progress
  • You prefer a personal shelf over storefront apps
  • Android folder pick brings a whole folder onto your shelf

Features

Shelf, reader, and settings that stay local

Everything below ships in the Expo app today.

  • Cover library

    Each comic shows its first page as cover art in a clean grid.

  • Continue reading

    Jump straight back to the last unfinished issue.

  • Organize

    Filter unread, reading, finished, or favorites. Sort by recent, title, or progress.

  • Folder import

    On Android, select a folder of CBZ/ZIP files and fill the shelf.

  • Double-page spreads

    Wide pages can auto-split in portrait when you enable it.

  • Reader chrome

    Scrubber, pinch zoom, brightness dimmer, and dark controls.

  • Comfort

    Keep the screen awake while reading; pages stay fit and centered.

  • Themes & storage

    Light, dark, or system. See library size and repair extracted pages.
